ABOUT DIGIMATE

About the Project

The idea behind DigiMate.

What is DigiMate?

DigiMate is an interactive digital-awareness platform designed to help secondary students understand digital services, technology and online safety in a simple and engaging way.

Main Goals

The project aims to increase digital awareness, encourage safe technology use and make learning about digital services more interactive.

Technologies Used

HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript are used to build the structure, visual design and interactive features of DigiMate.

How It Works

Explore → Learn → Challenge. Users explore digital topics, learn useful information and then test their knowledge through interactive challenges.
